Season 1: Kem Kem Group

Southeastern Morocco. A 100-million-year-old river system that was home to some of the largest predatory dinosaurs ever discovered. We're mapping its geological formations from orbit using Sentinel-2 multispectral imagery — building the first georeferenced training dataset for the Kem Kem's lithology.

Real Science

Spectral analysis of Sentinel-2 satellite bands detects iron oxide, carbonate, and clay mineral signatures. Random Forest classification maps geological formations. Every finding passes a three-stage validation pipeline.

AI Agents, Not AI Hype

15 specialized agents running on Google's Gemini 2.5 Flash. Each agent has a distinct role — from chief geologist to validation officer. A deterministic orchestrator tells them what to do. They don't hallucinate their way through; they call tools.

Measurable Self-Improvement

Six metrics tracked from day one. Classification accuracy, analysis throughput, finding validation rate, tool reuse, council decision quality, and multi-agent vs. single-agent comparison. The developmental trajectory claim is testable.
