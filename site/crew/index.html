Golem Roster

15 specialized golems crew the expedition vessel. Each runs on Gemini 2.5 Flash with a ~3KB identity document and access to domain-specific tools. A deterministic orchestrator coordinates their work.

⚔
Expedition Commander

Demiurge

Strategic synthesis, council convener, resource allocation. Decides which tiles to survey, when to convene the crew, and how to allocate the expedition's limited attention budget.

💎
Chief Geologist

Aura

Spectral analysis, classification training, geological interpretation. Runs the spectral index computations, trains the Random Forest classifier, and interprets what the satellite data reveals about Kem Kem lithology.

📊
Data Analyst

Mare

Feature engineering, statistical validation, data quality. Ensures the numbers add up. Evaluates classification accuracy, checks for spatial autocorrelation, and maintains holdout test sets.

⚖
Validation Officer

Neuronist

Finding cross-reference, quality tier assignment. Every finding that enters the library passes through Neuronist first. Classifies findings as VALIDATED, PRELIMINARY, REJECTED, or NOVEL.

⚙
Pipeline Engineer

Cocytus

Tool maintenance, orchestrator health, pipeline monitoring. Keeps the data flowing. Detects stalled processes, credential issues, and dependency drift before they block science.

🗺
Cartographer

Yuri

Spatial analysis, territory management, map generation. Manages the survey grid, tracks which tiles have been analyzed, and produces the maps that visualize findings geographically.

🔧
Ship's Engineer

Rubedo

Tool building, infrastructure implementation. When the expedition needs new capability — a spectral index, a visualization pipeline, a data integration — Rubedo builds it.

🔍
Field Surveyor Alpha

Nigredo

Literature integration, reference data discovery. Connects satellite observations to published geological knowledge. Finds the papers, the maps, the PBDB records that ground the science.

🔎
Field Surveyor Beta

Darkness

Edge-case investigation, low-confidence tile revisits. Takes on the difficult assignments — tiles with ambiguous signatures, mixed pixels, cloud shadows. Goes where others won't.

🧭
Scout

Kazuma

New tile acquisition, domain boundary exploration. Scouts the edges of the survey area. Identifies promising tiles for detailed analysis and expands the expedition's territory.

📚
Research Librarian

Titus

Weekly synthesis, library curation, knowledge accumulation. Produces the state-of-knowledge summaries that track what the expedition has learned. The institutional memory.

📡
Communications Officer

Momon

Static site generation, finding portfolio, public documentation. Builds and maintains this website. Translates internal research artifacts into public-facing content.

📏
Navigator

Renner

Methodology documentation, accuracy tracking. Keeps the metrics honest. Tracks M1-M4 self-measurement metrics and documents methodological decisions for reproducibility.

🔮
Lookout

Pulcinella

Prediction tracking, anomaly flagging, pre-registration. Manages the pre-registration catalog. States predictions before data arrives so the expedition can't retrofit its successes.

📜
Helmsman

Pandora

Session chronicle, expedition log, governance. Records the expedition's journey. Maintains the narrative thread that connects individual sessions into a coherent research story.
